Video to Audio Converter

Extract the audio track from a video and save it as an audio file.

No video selected

Conversion happens in real time while the video plays. The output format is WebM or OGG audio, depending on browser support. Where supported, you can choose a folder and save the file inside it; otherwise it downloads with your folder name as a filename prefix. Keep this tab open until conversion finishes.
